Living Ink Art

Thick outlines, halftone shading and speed lines. Switch colour palettes and the whole game, menus included, re-inks itself.

Real Drift Physics

Physics runs at a fixed 120 steps a second, so every slide is predictable and every run plays out the same way given the same inputs.

03
🏆

Weekly Rankings

A fresh leaderboard for every level, every week, on Google Play Games. Beat the CPU rivals and push them off the board.

My City: Race Your Streets

Type the name of any place, or use your location, and Manga Drift turns the real roads around it into a closed racing loop, with walls and hurdles. The track is saved on your phone, so you can race it offline afterwards. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
