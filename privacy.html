PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Summary What we collect Third parties Permissions Children Your choices Contact

This Privacy Policy explains how the Manga Drift mobile game ("Manga Drift", the "App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. The App's package name on Google Play is com.manva.mangadrift.

The short version

  • We do not run our own servers that collect your personal data, and we do not sell personal data.
  • Your progress, settings, purchases record and saved tracks are stored on your device.
  • Some features use services from Google and OpenStreetMap. Those services receive the data they need to work, as described below.
  • Location is optional. It is only used when you ask the My City feature to build a track near you.

Information the App uses

1. Data stored only on your device

Game progress (stars, unlocked levels, best scores), settings (controls, colour palette, car choice), your driver name, the record of your purchase and rewarded ads watched, cached leaderboards, and the My City tracks you create. This data stays in the App's private storage and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

2. Approximate location (optional)

If you choose "use my location" in My City, the App asks for permission to read your approximate (coarse) location. The coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to find the name of the place, and the surrounding area is sent to the Overpass service to download nearby streets. We do not store your location on any server of ours, and we do not track your location in the background. You can instead type a place name, in which case the place name you type is sent to Nominatim.

3. Google account name (optional, at purchase)

When you buy the Full Game, the App may offer Google Sign-In. If you sign in, the App reads the display name on your Google account and turns its first word into a driver name (for example "ALEX4821"). Only this driver name is kept, on your device. Sign-in is optional: if you cancel, the purchase still works.

4. Leaderboard scores

When you open the Rankings screen, your best weekly score per level and your player name may be submitted to Google Play Games Services, where they are visible to other players on that leaderboard. Nothing is sent before you open the Rankings screen.

5. Motion sensors and vibration

Tilt steering reads your phone's accelerometer while you are driving. These readings are used on the device only and are never stored or sent anywhere. Vibration is used for haptic feedback.

Android permissions

  • Internet: ads, purchases, leaderboards and downloading map data.
  • Approximate location: only for "use my location" in My City. You can refuse it and still play everything.
  • Billing: the Full Game purchase through Google Play.
  • Vibrate: haptic feedback.

Children's privacy

Manga Drift is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • Location: turn it off any time in Android Settings → Apps → Manga Drift → Permissions.
  • Ads personalisation: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Local data: clear the App's storage or uninstall it to delete everything stored on your device.
  • Google data: manage your Google and Play Games data at myaccount.google.com.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data. Because we do not hold your data on our own servers, most requests are best made directly to the services listed above, but you can always contact us for help.

Security and retention

Data on your device stays there until you delete it. Network requests made by the App use encrypted HTTPS connections. Third-party services keep data according to their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. The "Last updated" date at the top will show the latest version. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's Google Play release notes.
