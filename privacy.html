The fine print

PRIVACY
POLICY.

Your privacy matters. Here’s what happens to information you share with the Manga Drift website.

Information you provide

If you use our contact form, you choose to provide your name, email address, and message. We use that information only to receive and respond to your inquiry.

Contact form service

The contact form is processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service. Information you submit is sent through FormSubmit and delivered to the Manga Drift contact inbox. FormSubmit may process or retain information as described in its own privacy policy. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in your message.

Website hosting and cookies

This site does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. The website host may process basic technical information, such as request logs, to deliver and protect the site. The host’s own privacy terms apply to that processing.

Data retention and your choices

Messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and maintain correspondence. You may ask us to delete a message or its personal information by sending a request through the contact page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site or its services change. Any revision will be posted on this page.

Questions

For privacy questions or requests, please contact us.
